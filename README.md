# Shoot the Monkey Lab (Level 2)

This is the **Modification** level, on the SAMR model, of the [Shoot the Monkey](https://lallravi.github.io/shoot-the-monkey/) physics simulator.

Level 1 lets students explore the monkey-and-hunter puzzle. Level 2 redesigns the task so students work like scientists.

- **Quick checks on the slides.** Students record their first guess and answer a multiple-choice question on each concept, with feedback.
- **Predict, observe, explain.** The rifle won't fire until the student predicts hit or miss and gives a reason. After each shot the lab compares the prediction with the result and asks a question to explain it.
- **Data table and graph.** Every shot is logged. The dart's drop is plotted against t², with a best-fit line for each planet. Students measure g from the slope (g = 2 × slope). The table can be copied as CSV.
- **Six missions:**
  - predict three shots in a row correctly
  - find the slowest dart that still hits
  - hit the monkey on the Moon with a dart under 10 m/s
  - hit the monkey on Jupiter from 40 m or more
  - calculate the miss distance when the monkey holds on
  - measure g from the graph
- **Final explanation.** Students write why aiming straight works. Chips light up as they use the key ideas: gravity, same time, same fall and ½gt².

Students' work is saved in their own browser. **Start over** clears it for the next student.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step is needed.
